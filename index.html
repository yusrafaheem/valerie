Donation Audit

Upload photos of donated goods to produce a Word report. The report lists the items in the photos, the quantity of each, and a reference value taken from the Goodwill or Salvation Army valuation guide. Items not in either guide are priced from online listings where possible.

Values are estimates for reference only. They are not an appraisal or a tax determination.

1API key

Enter an Anthropic API key. Keys are created in the Claude Console. The key is sent only to api.anthropic.com.

Workspace ID (optional)

Required only if the key was created without a workspace. The ID is listed under Settings, Workspaces in the Claude Console.

2Photos

Up to 6 photos of one donation. Photos are numbered in the order they are added. Photos should not show people or personal information.

3Report details (optional)

Notes are included in the report as statements from the preparer. They are not verified.

4Run

Processing takes approximately 1 to 3 minutes. Online price searches add time.
